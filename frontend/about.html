UNITED NATIONS SDG 1

Goal 1: End Poverty in All Its Forms Everywhere

The NO POVERTY platform is a dedicated social-impact public digital platform designed to bridge information gaps between underprivileged Indian citizens and legitimate welfare entitlements.

Target 1.3

Implement nationally appropriate social protection systems and measures for all, and by 2030 achieve substantial coverage of the poor and vulnerable.

Target 1.4

Ensure that all men and women have equal rights to economic resources, basic services, ownership and control over land, and financial services.

Target 1.5

Build the resilience of the poor and reduce their exposure and vulnerability to climate-related extreme events and economic shocks.

Our Ethical Framework & Transparency

To maintain the highest standards of integrity and prevent fraud:

  • No Fake Submissions: This platform does NOT pretend to submit applications to government portals. All final application filings are directed strictly to official government portals (.gov.in or .nic.in).
  • Clear Eligibility Boundaries: Eligibility evaluations are advisory simulations based on user input. Official determinations rest solely with government field verification officers.
  • Privacy by Design: Citizen financial and identification documents are confidential and never sold or shared with commercial entities.
  • Multilingual Accessibility: Designed for accessibility across English, Hindi, Kannada, and Marathi with voice search and screen reading support.

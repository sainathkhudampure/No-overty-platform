DATA PROTECTION & PRIVACY

Citizen Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | UN SDG 1 Social Impact Platform

At NO POVERTY, protecting citizen personal, socio-economic, and document data is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy outlines how information is collected, stored, and utilized.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, check scheme eligibility, or store application numbers, we collect:

  • Basic identity data: Name, email address, mobile number, state, and district.
  • Socio-economic information: Annual income bracket, landholding status, student enrollment, and disability status.
  • Application tracking numbers: Acknowledgment numbers for your personal milestone tracking.
2. How Your Data Is Used

Information is used solely to:

  • Match relevant welfare programs and calculate eligibility probability.
  • Provide document readiness alerts and application milestone reminders.
  • Generate aggregate, non-identifiable public social welfare statistics.
3. No Sale of Citizen Data

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade citizen personal data to commercial advertisers or third-party marketing companies under any circumstances.

4. Document Security

Uploaded documents are stored in protected directories with access restricted strictly to your authenticated session.

OFFICIAL PORTAL ROADMAP

7-Step Official Application Guidance

Learn how to navigate official state and central Government of India portals to ensure your welfare entitlement is processed without rejection.

Step 1 — Verify Demographic & Income Eligibility

Use our Eligibility Wizard to ensure your annual family income and age bracket satisfy the criteria before beginning paperwork.

Step 2 — Prepare Digital Copies of Mandatory Documents

Ensure your Aadhaar is linked to your active DBT bank savings account and mobile number. Keep your income certificate, caste certificate, and land records in PDF/JPEG format (< 2MB).

Step 3 — Access Verified Government Portal

Never apply on third-party commercial portals. Only use official government domains ending in .gov.in or .nic.in.

Step 4 — Register via Aadhaar OTP Authentication

Create an account on the target ministry portal using your 12-digit Aadhaar number. Complete OTP mobile authentication or biometric e-KYC.

Step 5 — Submit Online Application Form

Carefully fill personal, educational, agricultural, or banking details. Double check your IFSC Code and bank account number to avoid payment transfer failures.

Step 6 — Download & Save Application Acknowledgement

Immediately download the PDF Acknowledgement Receipt containing your permanent Application Tracking Number.

Step 7 — Track Status in Application Tracker

Record your application number into our Application Tracker to keep track of verification milestones and officer inspections.
